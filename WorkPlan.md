## 14. Dual-gate execution semantics (normative, v0.4.3 C1–C3)

For every theorem node `L`, two distinct readiness predicates govern the two tracks. `NOT_REACHED` is always per-track, never whole-phase while the other track is runnable.

```text
REFUTE_READY(L) = frozen foundation + frozen exact statement/negation of L
  + survivor binding + exact executable semantics
  + conformance of L's applicable registered refutation interface, where the interface may be a PSC family, a dedicated theorem-specific attack interface, a formal exact-negation checker, or another preregistered interface explicitly assigned to L. No theorem requires membership in a PSC family merely to become REFUTE_READY. REFUTE_READY(L) further requires all theorem-specific frozen semantic/source dependencies of L, explicitly listed per L in theorem_battlefield (never generic infrastructure alone blocking a formal/source theorem).
PROVE_READY(L)  = REFUTE_READY(L) + every logical prerequisite of PROVE(L) REVIEWED.
```

- `REFUTE(L)` may execute whenever `REFUTE_READY(L)` holds — including `REFUTE(MST0-14)` under unreviewed upstreams, `REFUTE(MST0-15)` under unreviewed MST0-14, and `REFUTE(MST0-17)` under unreviewed upstreams (its endpoint-aware negation instantiates from frozen statement + semantics alone).
- `PROVE(L)` may execute only whenever `PROVE_READY(L)` holds: Phase-3 PROVE needs all five upstream REVIEWED; Phase-4 PROVE needs MST0-14 REVIEWED; Phase-5 PROVE needs per-node readiness — 17 needs all seven upstream REVIEWED, 18 needs MST0-17 REVIEWED, 19 needs MST0-18 REVIEWED plus frozen bridge sources and conventions (v0.4.4 D1: 7 upstream → 17 → 18 → 19, never compressed).
- Exact `REFUTED(L)` freezes later POSITIVE-route consumption and, for eligible obstructions, ACTIVATES Phase 6. Proof failure, human REJECT, or BLOCKED never equals `REFUTED` without an exact negation witness (v0.4.1 A2).
- Per-track gates from §12-H apply independently: a closed PROVE gate marks that track `NOT_REACHED`; the REFUTE track in the same phase is unaffected.
