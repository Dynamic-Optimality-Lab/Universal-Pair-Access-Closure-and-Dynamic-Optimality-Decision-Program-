## 2. No-model-training, brutal-benchmark, and anti-overfitting policy

There are **no ML models to train** in v0.4. This section states that explicitly and then gives the exact analogue policy the user requested (training specifics → benchmarks entirely different from training → anti-overfitting actions), instantiated for this theorem-decision program.

**2.1 No training.**
- `MSTC-0002` is immutable experimental input (§1.2). No gradient fitting, hyperparameter search, constant ladder, grammar search, credit-type search, predicate search, or threshold tuning is permitted on the theorem path. The v0.3 diagnostic constant ladder is explicitly not run in v0.4 (§1.5).
- Proof-attack generators (`locality_explosion.py`, `primitive_exhaust.py`, `boundary_torture.py`, `k6_saturation.py`, `double_spend.py`, `pair_access_search.py`, `telescope_torture.py`, `lift_obstruction.py`) are deterministic exact falsification instruments, not learned models. - If any auxiliary statistical tool is ever used for conjecture suggestion, it is quarantined as theorem-mining only (§1.3 role 2), never as proof, with full disclosure in `AI_USE.md`.

**2.2 Brutal benchmarks entirely different from any “training” data.**
- The only “training-adjacent” finite evidence is the sealed v0.3 corpus (dev episodes + consumed `HOLDOUT-H3T-v0.3` bank, 70,000 episodes, 7 sizes, 11 strata + large-n sweep `16..256` + clean-room replay). **None of it is reused as a benchmark.** H3T is `UNLOCKED_ONCE/1` and never re-unlocked or regenerated (`STOP-08/09`, `INV-012`, `T007`); sibling kills are preserved read-only; finite survival is never a premise (`INV-014`, `T008/09`).
- Every WorkPlan phase that attacks a theorem uses **fresh, negation-derived, entirely independent benchmarks**:
  - `PSC-L/P/B/K6/I/T/N` families generated from exact theorem negations (Phase 1 frozen spec + conformance), with deterministic schedules `16,24,32,48,64,96,128,192,256,384,512,768,1024,…` (§26.1) plus symbolic parameter families (arbitrary intervals/sizes/ranks/supports) and exact constraint/SMT solving where possible (§26.2) — independently generated from v0.3 banks (different generators, seeds, objectives, canonical order, logical-stream hashes); canonical episode hashes are compared to exclude reuse where v0.3 bank bytes are available, otherwise stated as independently generated (zero overlap is not claimed by prose alone).
